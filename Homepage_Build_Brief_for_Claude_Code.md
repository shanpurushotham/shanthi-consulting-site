## Homepage copy (final — V6)

### HERO
**Headline:** I help startups build the company around the product, right after they raise.
**Subhead:** You've got traction and real funding for the first time. I help you build the operating foundation: the systems, hiring, and operating rhythm that hold as you grow. I've built it twice. I can help you build yours.

### WHO THIS IS FOR
**Lead line:** The product works. Investors believed in it. And somehow, everything still runs through you.

This is for you if:
- The team is growing fast, and responsibilities need to get out of your head and into real systems.
- You have money for the first time, and you genuinely don't know how to allocate it without wasting it.
- You need to hire — but at the right cadence and the right roles, not just fast.
- You need real transparency into what's happening across teams as you move from a handful of people toward sixty or seventy.

**Closing line:** If that's the season you're in, let's talk.

### WHAT I DO
- **Operating systems setup** — OKRs, quarterly planning, and the operating cadence that keeps leadership aligned as you grow.
- **Fundraising and investor readiness** — I've supported a $500K seed, a $13.5M Series A, and a $5M subsidiary raise, and sat across the table during diligence.
- **Org design and hiring** — who to hire, when, and how to avoid the expensive early mistakes.
- **Impact, Analytics, and KPI tracking** — real-time dashboards that give leadership visibility into performance, budget, and team health, even after you can no longer track it all yourself.

### ABOUT — Hey, I'm Shanthi.
I was standing next to the CEO when we scaled from 5 people to 60, right after we closed a $14M Series A.

I learned how not to waste resources — where to put your budget, where not to, which marketing bets to make first and when to scale them, and how to hire without burning either money or morale.

The hardest part isn't the growth itself — it's that what your leadership team needs at 5 people is completely different from what it needs at 100. Past a certain size, your leaders can't keep a personal pulse on everything anymore, so your systems have to do that work instead: real-time, visible to everyone, understood across every team. Skip that work and the cost is real — wasted money, lost morale, and hires made too fast or in the wrong seats, when your people are the foundation the whole company sits on.

**Heart and rigor.** Before startups, I did this same kind of work in global health — two $20M programs launched across Sub-Saharan Africa, a $300M portfolio managed across 15+ countries, systems built inside a refugee camp in Bangladesh. When a broken process means someone doesn't get care, you learn to take the details seriously. I bring that same standard to your company.

I've done this twice now. I'd like to do it for you next.
